Kept: candidate-linking test

Gives the model a note and a short list of existing commitments, and checks which commitment it attaches the note to. Each case runs twice, with the list in two orders. Nothing is saved to the store.

Each answer is scored twice: as the model returned it, and after the app rules, which turn a contradictory answer into a question for the user. "pass" is the expected answer. "question" means nothing wrong would be proposed, but the user is asked to choose. "unsafe" means a wrong change would be proposed or one is lost.
